---
title: About the name
description: Why it's "OpenSSF Scorecard", singular.
weight: 1
---

The project was first called "Security Scorecards", but the repository and
the program were always named `scorecard`. To end the mix of singular and
plural, the project settled on **OpenSSF Scorecard**, or **Scorecard** for
short.

The logo was inspired by
["You passed! All D's ... and an A!"](https://youtu.be/rDMMYT3vkTk)
