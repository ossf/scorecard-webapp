---

Scorecard helps maintainers check a new project before launch and plan
improvements to an existing one.

1. Add the [GitHub Action](/get-started/github-action/) so every change is
   checked.
2. Work through the [beginner's guide](/docs/beginners-guide/), which picks
   the checks with the biggest payoff for the least effort.
3. Turn on `publish_results` and add a [badge](/get-started/badges/) to show
   your work.
